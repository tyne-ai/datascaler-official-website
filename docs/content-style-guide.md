# DataScaler 官网文案指南

中英文是两套面向不同市场的文案，不做逐句对译。所有产品声明都应能用现有功能或数据证明。

## 中文

- 默认读者是中国出海品牌的市场、用户研究和产品团队。
- 用“你”和“团队”，少用“您”。句子尽量短，先说用户问题，再说产品能做什么。
- 优先使用国内团队熟悉的说法，例如“原帖”“测评”“售后反馈”“购买顾虑”。
- 避免“赋能”“全域”“增长引擎”“机会窗口”“心智”等抽象词，除非它们是必要的行业概念。
- 不中英混写同一个标题或标签；品牌名、平台名和通用缩写除外。

## English (US)

- Write for US-based brand, consumer insights, marketing, and product teams.
- Lead with the customer problem or outcome. Use short sentences, active voice, and contractions where they sound natural.
- Prefer concrete product language: `source post`, `customer objection`, `review trend`, `support data`, and `next step`.
- Avoid generic SaaS filler such as `unlock`, `empower`, `revolutionize`, `seamless`, `actionable insights`, and `growth engine`.
- Do not mirror Chinese sentence structure. Rewrite the idea in natural US English.

## Claims and numbers

- Do not use `100%`, predicted lift, propagation probability, or compliance absolutes without documented evidence.
- Keep plan limits, source coverage, and credit validity consistent across the homepage, pricing page, FAQ, and product.
- Legal copy should describe current practice without promising facts that have not been confirmed. Changes to governing law, contracting entity, retention, or liability terms require legal review.
